From Metro Philadelphia: Reading Terminal Market’s Ice Cream Festival is returning to Philadelphia for another tasty celebration. Set for Saturday, Aug. 15, from noon to 4 p.m., the market will once again transform the 1100 block of Filbert Street into one of the largest community ice cream festivals in the country.
